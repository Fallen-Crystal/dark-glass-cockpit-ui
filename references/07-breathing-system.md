# Breathing System

Use a single requestAnimationFrame controller to generate a master phase.

## Modes

- off
- gentle
- standard
- showcase

## Driven layers

- main panel scale/brightness
- topbar field scale/lift/edge
- compact data rows/groups
- timeline
- focused 3D card
- ambient stage halo
- sparse particles
- fluid card shell/body/edge

## Fluid rhythms

### sync
All cards share the same phase.

### stagger
Each card receives a phase offset.

### layered
Middle/focal cards form stronger peaks while outer cards form lower secondary ridges. Use asymmetric phase/amplitude to avoid mechanical repetition.

Respect reduced motion and make the entire system disable-able.
