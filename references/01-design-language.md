# Design Language

## Keywords

Dark, restrained, translucent, acrylic, low-light, cinematic, information-dense, precise.

## Material hierarchy

1. Black/near-black environmental background.
2. Main translucent glass planes.
3. Low-contrast nested controls.
4. Local accent states and focused objects.
5. Optional ambient effects behind, never above, critical content.

## Do

- keep contrast hierarchy obvious;
- use thin low-alpha borders;
- place glow close to the selected object;
- keep whitespace disciplined rather than oversized;
- use a small number of radii and type weights.

## Don't

- opaque charcoal card grids;
- white milky glass;
- thick neon rims;
- large saturated gradients;
- decorative icon soup;
- equally animated every element.
